What are Meta Tags?

Meta tags are HTML elements placed in the <head> section of a web page. They provide metadata — information about the page — to search engines, social media platforms, and browsers. While invisible to visitors, meta tags are critical for SEO and social sharing because they control how your page appears in search results and link previews.

Most Important Meta Tags for SEO

  • Title tag (<title>) — The single most important on-page SEO element. It appears as the clickable headline in search results. Optimal length: 50-60 characters.
  • Meta description — The snippet shown below the title in search results. Not a direct ranking factor, but heavily influences click-through rate. Optimal length: 150-160 characters.
  • Canonical URL — Tells search engines the preferred URL for a page, preventing duplicate content issues when the same page is accessible via multiple URLs.
  • Robots — Controls whether search engines should index the page and follow its links. The default is index, follow.

Open Graph Tags Explained

Open Graph (OG) is a protocol created by Facebook that lets you control how your content appears when shared on social media. When someone pastes your URL on Facebook, LinkedIn, or messaging apps, the platform reads your OG tags to build the share preview card — showing the image, title, and description you specify.

The four essential OG tags are: og:title, og:description, og:image (recommended: 1200×630px), and og:type.

Twitter Card Tags

Twitter Cards work similarly to Open Graph but use their own tag format (twitter:card, twitter:title, etc.). The two most common card types are summary (small square image) and summary_large_image (wide banner image above title). If Twitter Card tags are not present, Twitter falls back to reading Open Graph tags.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are meta tags?

Meta tags are HTML elements in the <head> section that provide metadata about a web page to search engines, social platforms, and browsers. They control how your page appears in search results and social shares. The most important are the title tag and meta description.

What is the ideal title tag length?

Google typically displays 50-60 characters of a title tag in search results. Titles longer than ~60 characters may be truncated with an ellipsis. Aim for 50-60 characters and put the most important keywords first.

What are Open Graph tags?

Open Graph tags (og:title, og:description, og:image, og:type) control how your page appears when shared on Facebook, LinkedIn, and other social platforms. They define the title, description, and image shown in the share preview card. The recommended image size is 1200×630 pixels.

Do Twitter Cards fall back to Open Graph?

Yes. If you have Open Graph tags but no Twitter Card tags, Twitter will use your OG title, description, and image. However, you still need to specify twitter:card to choose the card type (summary vs summary_large_image). Adding explicit Twitter tags gives you more control.

Are keywords meta tags still useful for SEO?

Google has explicitly stated it ignores the keywords meta tag for ranking purposes since 2009. However, some other search engines (Yandex, Baidu) may still consider them. Including keywords is harmless but should not be your focus — invest effort in title and description instead.
