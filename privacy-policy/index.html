1. Introduction

DevToolsHub (devtoolshub.pages.dev) provides free, browser-based developer tools including JSON formatters, code minifiers, encoders, generators, and other utilities. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit and use our website.

By using DevToolsHub, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the website.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Data We Do NOT Collect

We want to be absolutely clear about what we do not collect:

  • No personal data — We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any personally identifiable information (PII).
  • No tool input data — Any text, code, files, or data you enter into our tools is processed entirely in your browser and is never sent to any server.
  • No user accounts — We do not require registration, login, or authentication of any kind.
  • No file uploads to servers — Files you upload (e.g., CSV files, images for Base64 encoding) are read locally using the browser's File API and never leave your device.

2.2 Data We May Collect

Through third-party services (Google Analytics and Google AdSense), the following anonymous data may be collected:

  • Page views and navigation patterns — Which tools are visited and how users navigate the site.
  • Device and browser information — Browser type, operating system, screen resolution, and device category (desktop, mobile, tablet).
  • Geographic region — Country-level location derived from IP address (IP addresses are anonymised).
  • Referral source — How you arrived at DevToolsHub (search engine, direct link, social media).

3. Client-Side Processing

All DevToolsHub tools are implemented in pure client-side JavaScript. This means:

  • All processing happens inside your web browser on your own device.
  • No data is transmitted over the network when you use a tool.
  • Your input data exists only in your browser's memory and is discarded when you close the tab or navigate away.
  • We have no ability to see, access, or recover any data you enter into our tools.

This architecture was chosen deliberately to provide maximum privacy and security. You can verify this by using your browser's Developer Tools (Network tab) to confirm that no data is sent when you use any tool.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

4.1 Local Storage (First-Party)

We use the browser's localStorage API to save your theme preference (dark mode or light mode). This data:

  • Is stored only on your device.
  • Is never sent to any server.
  • Contains only the key devtoolshub-theme with a value of "dark" or "light".
  • Can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

4.2 Third-Party Cookies

Our third-party service providers may set cookies on your device:

  • Google Analytics cookies (_ga, _gid, _gat) — Used to distinguish unique users and throttle request rates. These cookies expire after 2 years, 24 hours, and 1 minute respectively.
  • Google AdSense cookies — Used to serve relevant advertisements and track ad interactions. These cookies may be used to build an interest profile for personalised advertising.

5. Third-Party Services

5.1 Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use DevToolsHub, which helps us improve our tools and user experience. Google Analytics collects anonymous usage data and does not identify individual users.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google Analytics' Data Privacy and Security.

You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5.2 Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on DevToolsHub. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites on the internet.

For more information, see How Google Uses Cookies in Advertising.

5.3 Cloudflare Pages

DevToolsHub is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process connection data (IP addresses, request headers) to provide hosting, CDN, and security services. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

6. Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on DevToolsHub. These ads help us keep all tools free for everyone.

  • Google may use cookies to serve advertisements based on your browsing history and interests.
  • Third-party ad networks and vendors may also use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to serve ads.
  • We do not control the cookies or tracking technologies used by advertisers.

Opt out of personalised advertising:

  • Google Ads Settings — Manage your ad personalisation preferences.
  • Digital Advertising Alliance Opt-Out — Opt out of interest-based advertising from participating companies.
  • Your Online Choices (EU) — European opt-out tool for behavioural advertising.

7. Data Retention

Since we do not collect personal data through our tools, there is no personal data to retain. The anonymous analytics data collected by Google Analytics is retained according to Google's data retention policies. Your theme preference stored in localStorage persists until you clear your browser data.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Right to access — You can view your localStorage data through your browser's Developer Tools.
  • Right to deletion — You can clear all locally stored data by clearing your browser's site data for DevToolsHub.
  • Right to opt out — You can opt out of analytics tracking and personalised advertising using the links provided above.
  • Right to object — You can use ad-blocking extensions or disable JavaScript to prevent any third-party scripts from loading.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), these rights are granted under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). For users in California, these rights are provided under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

9. Children's Privacy

DevToolsHub is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Since our tools do not require any form of registration or data input that is transmitted to our servers, we believe our services pose minimal privacy risk to users of any age. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has somehow provided personal information through our site, please contact us so we can address the issue.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. When we make changes:

  • The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised.
  • Material changes will be highlighted on the page for a reasonable period.
  • Continued use of DevToolsHub after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

We recommend reviewing this page periodically to stay informed about how we protect your privacy.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests related to this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us:

  • Email: contact@devtoolshub.com
  • Website: DevToolsHub Contact Page

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 30 days.
