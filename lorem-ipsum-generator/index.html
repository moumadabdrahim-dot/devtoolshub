What is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum is the industry-standard placeholder text used in design, typesetting, and web development. It provides a block of text that looks like natural language — with realistic word lengths, line breaks, and letter distribution — without being actual readable content. This lets designers and developers focus on visual layout rather than content meaning.

The most recognisable opening — "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit" — has been the typesetting industry's standard dummy text ever since the 1500s, when an unknown printer scrambled a section of Cicero's philosophical work to make a type specimen book.

History of Lorem Ipsum

Contrary to popular belief, Lorem Ipsum is not random gibberish. It has roots in a piece of classical Latin literature from 45 BC — over 2,000 years old. Richard McClintock, a Latin professor at Hampden-Sydney College in Virginia, traced the text to sections 1.10.32 and 1.10.33 of "De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum" (On the Ends of Good and Evil) by Marcus Tullius Cicero.

The original passage discusses hedonism and pain — the theory that pleasure is the highest good. Over the centuries, the text was corrupted, with words altered, added, and removed, producing the nonsensical Latin we use today. The modern version has been the standard since the 1960s, when Letraset released transfer sheets containing Lorem Ipsum passages, and later when Aldus PageMaker included it as default sample text.

Why Use Placeholder Text?

  • Focus on design, not content — Placeholder text prevents stakeholders from getting distracted by reading actual content during design reviews. It keeps attention on typography, spacing, and layout.
  • Realistic text flow — Unlike "Content here, content here" repeated text, Lorem Ipsum mimics real language patterns, giving a more accurate visual impression of the final layout.
  • Test responsive layouts — Variable paragraph and word lengths help you stress-test how your design handles different content volumes.
  • Industry standard — Lorem Ipsum is universally recognised by designers, developers, and clients as placeholder content, eliminating confusion about final copy.
  • Available before copywriting — Design often begins before content is written. Lorem Ipsum fills the gap so design work is not blocked by content production.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum is dummy placeholder text used in the design, typesetting, and web development industries. It originates from a 1st-century BC Latin text by Cicero called "De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum." It provides natural-looking text distribution without meaningful content, allowing designers to focus on visual appearance.

Why use Lorem Ipsum instead of real text?

Using real text in early design stages distracts reviewers — they focus on reading the content instead of evaluating the design. Lorem Ipsum's Latin roots make it obviously placeholder text while maintaining realistic word lengths, sentence rhythms, and letter frequencies that closely match English text distribution.

Is Lorem Ipsum real Latin?

Partially. The original text derives from sections 1.10.32 and 1.10.33 of Cicero's "De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum" (45 BC). However, over centuries the text was scrambled — words were altered, added, and removed — so modern Lorem Ipsum is not grammatically correct Latin. It is recognisably Latin-like but ultimately nonsensical.

Can I use Lorem Ipsum in production?

No — Lorem Ipsum is strictly for development and design stages. Before going live, you should replace all placeholder text with final, real content. Search engines may penalise pages containing Lorem Ipsum, and users will lose trust if they encounter placeholder text on a live site.
